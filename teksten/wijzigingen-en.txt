Changes in Paper Pilot

Version 0.0.0.7 (November 2026)
- Bigger letters in the reading window with Ctrl+plus (smaller with Ctrl+minus), for people who are visually impaired. The manual follows that size too.
- Help menu, Keyboard shortcuts: all shortcuts in one list.
- PDFs that Paper Pilot makes now have a title and a language (better for screen readers).
- New: Check, for sighted people who make a PDF, Word document or website. Paper Pilot tells you in plain language what a screen reader cannot read well and how to fix it.
- New: reminders (Ctrl+R and the File menu). Paper Pilot warns you from three days before, even when the program is not open.
- New: My post (File menu). All letters you read, with date and sender, so you can find them again. Encrypted on your own computer; can be turned off.
- New: My signature (Settings menu). Scan it once; after that Paper Pilot puts it on forms.
- New: email as an attachment (Ctrl+M), also after saving a filled-in form.
- New: What do I need to do? (Ctrl+T). A short summary of the letter (with the language model on your own computer), and the amount, account number, payment reference, deadline and phone number to copy.
- New: reading QR codes and barcodes on scans, photos and PDFs. Payment QR codes become a payment request. Open links and copy details with Ctrl+Q.
- New: open more kinds of files: old Word (.doc), RTF, OpenDocument (.odt, .ods, .odp), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), web pages (.html) and e-books (.epub). Use Ctrl+K to go to a chapter.
- New: fill in PDF forms, question by question (Ctrl+I in the reading window). Text fields, check boxes, radio buttons and lists.
- Paper Pilot finds the question that belongs to each field, and tells you when a question is required.
- Overview of all questions and answers (Ctrl+L), and save as a new PDF (Ctrl+S). The answers can be seen in every PDF reader.
- New: My details (Settings menu). Name, address, date of birth, phone, email, BSN and IBAN are filled in for you in forms. Stored encrypted on your own computer.
- After saving, Paper Pilot asks whether new details may be kept in My details.
- Also forms without form fields: PDFs with only lines and boxes, and scanned forms. Paper Pilot finds the places itself: a question with a colon and empty space, lines, dots and boxes. The answers are put in the right place, with a cross in the checked boxes.

Version 0.0.0.6 (November 2026)
- New: recognising envelopes, also window envelopes. Paper Pilot tells you who the post is from (logo, return address or a known sender) and who it is addressed to.
- Small print is read better, such as medicine leaflets, labels and tea bag tags: Paper Pilot enlarges the picture and increases the contrast when that works better.
- When scanning you choose what you scan: a letter, an envelope, or something small with small print (then Paper Pilot scans at high resolution, 600 dpi).

Version 0.0.0.5 (November 2026)
- New: scanning (Ctrl+N). Paper Pilot finds scanners itself: connected, on the network, and the scanner in your printer. Also network printers with a scanner that Windows does not know yet (eSCL/AirScan).
- Scan from the glass (page by page) or from the document feeder (all pages at once). Paper Pilot then recognises the text.
- New: printing (Ctrl+P) on any printer Windows knows: the text, or the scan or photo.
- New: save as a PDF with the text in it (Ctrl+Shift+S). The PDF can then be searched and read aloud later.
- Read and fill in first asks: open a file, or scan.

Version 0.0.0.4 (November 2026)
- New: read handwriting (Ctrl+H), such as shopping lists and postcards.
- New: describe what is on a photo, drawing or scan (Ctrl+D).
- This is done by a language model on your own computer, without the internet. You download the model once (about 3.5 GB), if you choose to. You can remove it via Settings.
- The download shows a progress bar, can be stopped, and later continues where it stopped.
- The privacy statement now also mentions the language model download.

Version 0.0.0.3 (November 2026)
- New: text recognition. Scanned letters and photos become text, without the internet (using Windows text recognition).
- Scanned PDF files can now be read as well.
- Paper Pilot tells you when a scan is upside down, on its side or crooked, and straightens it.
- Open all common picture files: JPG, PNG, BMP, GIF, TIFF (also with several pages), and HEIC and WebP when the Windows extension is installed.
- Save the straightened scan as a picture (Ctrl+Shift+S): PNG, JPG, TIFF, BMP or GIF.

Version 0.0.0.2 (November 2026)
- New: reading documents. Choose Read and fill in and open a PDF, a Word document (.docx) or a text file.
- Paper Pilot turns it into clean text, with the pages and the headings. Hyphenated words are joined again.
- In the reading window: Find (Ctrl+F, F3 and Shift+F3), Headings (Ctrl+K), Go to page (Ctrl+G) and Save as text (Ctrl+S).
- Paper Pilot tells you when a document is a form, and when a page has no text (a scan).
- In File Explorer you can open a PDF or Word document with Paper Pilot (Open with).
- While you hold down the Insert key or Caps Lock (your screen reader keys), Paper Pilot does not react to any key.
- Checking for updates now works properly.

Version 0.0.0.1 (November 2026)
- The first version: the basics of Paper Pilot.
- Main window with the two parts Read and fill in and Check (these will come in the next versions).
- Menu bar; everything works with the keyboard and with the mouse.
- Dutch and English (Settings menu, Language / Taal).
- Appearance: automatic (like Windows), light or dark; Windows high contrast is followed.
- Manual (F1), guided tour, changes and privacy statement in the Help menu.
- Mail the maker and More programs by Jaap.
- Updates: Paper Pilot checks once a day for a new version and installs it if you want.
- Installed in Program Files; uninstall via the Start menu or Windows Settings.
