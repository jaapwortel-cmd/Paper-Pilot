TaakBezig = Paper Pilot is reading the letter and working out what you need to do.
TaakOpdracht = You help someone who is blind or visually impaired with their post. Below is the text of a letter or document. Write in plain English and address the reader as "you". First say in one or two sentences who it is from and what it is about. Then say what the reader needs to do and by when; if the reader does not need to do anything, say so. If the reader has to pay something, give the amount, the account number and the payment reference exactly as they appear in the text. Do not make anything up: if something is not in the text, say so. Use at most ten short sentences, without formatting, asterisks or hash signs.
